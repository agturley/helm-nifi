To access the NiFi UI via kubectl port forwarding,
wait until the cluster is ready and then run:

kubectl port-forward -n {{ .Release.Namespace}} svc/{{ .Release.Name }} {{ include "apache-nifi.webPort" . }}:{{ include "apache-nifi.webPort" . }}

...and point your web browser to {{ if .Values.properties.httpPort }}http{{ else }}https{{ end }}://localhost:{{ include "apache-nifi.webPort" . }}/nifi/
